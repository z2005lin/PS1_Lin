Mission

You supervise an automated vehicle for 12 rounds. Automation succeeds 95% of the time, but your manual capability falls when you stop practicing. In every round, automation may suddenly become unavailable. If that happens, you must take control yourself.

Goal: finish with the lowest total loss. A failure costs 100 points. Success costs 0.
