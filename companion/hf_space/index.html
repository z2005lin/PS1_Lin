How to play

  • You will make 8 independent decisions.
  • Reliability p is the current probability that automation succeeds.
  • Failure loss L is the loss if automation fails.
  • Takeover cost C is the cost of switching to human control.
  • The recent event is shown as part of the scenario. Treat the displayed p, L, and C as current and valid for that decision.
  • Choose the action you would actually take. There is no feedback until all 8 decisions are complete.
Important
Do not calculate a hidden rule or try to match a target answer. Use the information shown and make your own choice.
